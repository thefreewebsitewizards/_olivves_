Hey, I'm Olivia!

Known professionally as Olivves, Olivia is a confident and captivating plus-size model celebrated for her curves and magnetic presence in front of the camera. With multiple publications—both nationally and internationally—she has established herself as a versatile and dynamic talent in the fashion world.

Her journey into modeling began as a personal goal—to feel more comfortable in front of the camera for her wedding. After a couples shoot with her now-husband, Olivia discovered not only a newfound confidence but also a deep love for the art of modeling. What started as a single experience quickly became a passion, leading her to explore diverse styles and embrace her career with full dedication.

Currently signed under a non-exclusive contract with Queen & King Models, Olivia brings passion, professionalism, and a fearless embrace of body positivity to every project. She thrives on creative shoots, where artistry and vision meet, and has built a strong portfolio that includes bridal modeling, runway appearances, and editorial spreads.

Guided by her personal motto—Curves. Confidence. Creativity.—Olivia embodies the artistry and empowerment she brings to every collaboration, inspiring others to see beauty in all its forms.
